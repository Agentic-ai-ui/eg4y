# 15 · Brand and legal boundaries (`BRD`)

> Part of the **Apple Design Language** rule set — Created by Edison Augustin X.
> Derived from [`GUIDELINES.md`](../GUIDELINES.md) §18. Format and severities: [`README.md`](README.md).

This package teaches agents to design **for** the Apple ecosystem — never to impersonate Apple or clone its devices, apps, or branding. These rules are non-negotiable.

---

### BRD-01 · No Apple trademarks in names or images
- **Level:** MUST NOT
- **Severity:** error
- **Platforms:** All
- **Check:** review
- **Rule:** MUST NOT use Apple trademarks in an app name or images; follow Apple’s trademark guidelines.
- **Source:** [HIG › Branding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding) · [GUIDELINES §18](../GUIDELINES.md#18-brand-and-legal-boundaries)

### BRD-02 · No replicas of Apple hardware
- **Level:** MUST NOT
- **Severity:** error
- **Platforms:** All
- **Check:** review
- **Rule:** MUST NOT reproduce Apple hardware in app icons, custom symbols, or artwork; when Apple devices must appear, use only Apple Design Resources images or the SF Symbols that represent Apple products.
- **Source:** [HIG › App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) · [HIG › Icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/icons) · [HIG › SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sf-symbols) · [GUIDELINES §18](../GUIDELINES.md#18-brand-and-legal-boundaries)

### BRD-03 · Respect SF Symbols terms
- **Level:** MUST NOT
- **Severity:** error
- **Platforms:** All
- **Check:** review
- **Rule:** MUST NOT use SF Symbols — or confusingly similar images — in app icons, logos, or any trademarked use, and MUST NOT customize symbols that depict Apple products or features.
- **Source:** [HIG › SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sf-symbols) · [GUIDELINES §18](../GUIDELINES.md#18-brand-and-legal-boundaries)

### BRD-04 · Never imitate system UI
- **Level:** MUST NOT
- **Severity:** error
- **Platforms:** All
- **Check:** review
- **Rule:** MUST NOT build custom window chrome that replicates system windows or screens that imitate system permission or tracking alerts.
- **Source:** [HIG › Windows](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/windows) · [HIG › Privacy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy) · [GUIDELINES §18](../GUIDELINES.md#18-brand-and-legal-boundaries)

### BRD-05 · Let branding defer to content
- **Level:** SHOULD
- **Severity:** warning
- **Platforms:** All
- **Check:** review
- **Rule:** SHOULD express brand through voice, a judicious accent color, content-layer color, an optional custom headline font, and the app icon — not by displaying logos throughout the app or restyling familiar components beyond recognition.
- **Source:** [HIG › Branding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding) · [GUIDELINES §18](../GUIDELINES.md#18-brand-and-legal-boundaries)
